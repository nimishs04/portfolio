§1 About

I'm a PhD student in the Department of Materials Science and Engineering at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), working on the light-matter interaction of chiral metasurfaces. I completed my integrated BS-MS in Physics at IISER Kolkata, where my thesis focused on enhancing chiroptical signals in nanoparticles via structured light. I'm passionate about bridging nanophotonics, chiral optics, and computational electromagnetics. Apart from academics, I never miss an opportunity for an evening of football, and I play fingerstyle guitar and volunteer in my free time.

Research interests

  • Light-matter interaction in chiral metasurfaces and nanophotonic structures
  • Enhancing and exploiting chiroptical responses in nanoparticles and structured materials via structured light
  • Bridging computational electromagnetics with experimental optics — Mie scattering theory, Mueller matrix analysis, polarization-resolved microscopy
  • Spin-orbit interactions and optical spin Hall effects in metasurfaces and plasmonic crystals
  • Quantum optics for information processing — chiral and on-chip quantum optics, quantum communication, quantum sensing
  • Nanoscale quantum control, including single- and multi-photon emitters and strong light-matter coupling
